Before you ask

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting separately?

Yes. The site is separate from your domain and hosting, which are inexpensive ongoing services. I will point you to good options and help you get set up.

What if I do not like the free preview?

Then we stop there. No charge and no obligation. The preview exists so you only pay for something you actually want.

Can I get extra pages or more languages?

Yes, both are possible. Anything beyond the agreed scope is quoted before work starts, so there are no surprises.

How do overseas clients pay?

Australian bank transfer, or Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana if that is easier from where you are.

How long does it take?

It depends on how quickly the content comes together. Most of the wait is usually gathering photos, text and details rather than the build itself.

Can you change it later?

Yes. You own the files, so you can come back to me or take them to anyone else. There is no platform holding the site hostage.
